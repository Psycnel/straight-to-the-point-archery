About Straight to the Point Archery

Building skills, confidence, and community through archery since 2005.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide safe, high-quality archery instruction for students of all ages and skill levels. We are committed to building confidence, discipline, and focus through the sport of archery.

Our History

Founded in 2005, Straight to the Point Archery has been serving the Tacoma community for years. What started as a small training space has grown into a well-known archery education center dedicated to excellence and safety.

Our Facilities

Our facility features modern equipment, a safe indoor range, and a welcoming environment for all students. We ensure that all equipment is maintained to the highest standards to provide the best learning experience.

Our Commitment

Straight to the Point Archery is committed to providing an inclusive and respectful environment. We welcome students of all backgrounds and ensure equal opportunities for everyone to learn and grow.
